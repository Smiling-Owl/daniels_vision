# Third-party data and attribution

## StatsBomb Open Data

This project analyzes StatsBomb Open Data for La Liga 2015/2016 (competition 11, season 27), pinned to [`hudl/open-data` revision `4b73468fc5b0f1950f9f66fada70ad3a4f9327cb`](https://github.com/hudl/open-data/tree/4b73468fc5b0f1950f9f66fada70ad3a4f9327cb). The upstream [README](https://github.com/hudl/open-data/blob/4b73468fc5b0f1950f9f66fada70ad3a4f9327cb/README.md) asks publications to identify StatsBomb as the data source and use its logo. The project README displays the unmodified official logo hosted at the same pinned revision; the logo remains StatsBomb's property and is used for attribution.

The pinned [StatsBomb Public Data User Agreement](https://github.com/hudl/open-data/blob/4b73468fc5b0f1950f9f66fada70ad3a4f9327cb/LICENSE.pdf) says users may not distribute, reproduce, sell, or otherwise provide the data to third parties (§1.2.1), and may not commercially exploit the data or analysis derived from it (§1.2.2). It allows public sharing of research and analysis subject to its terms and requires the StatsBomb logo on publications based on the data (§1.4). It also asks users to register interest before accessing the service (§2.2). Review the complete agreement and follow the provider's current instructions before acquiring or publishing material.

This repository does not include the raw event JSON, the complete processed shot table, per-shot prediction CSVs, or fitted `.joblib` estimators. Those generated artifacts are excluded by `.gitignore`; source scripts recreate them locally. Aggregate tables, figures, and report/presentation outputs are retained as the project's research analysis.

The report also contains university and department marks supplied for the academic report format; those marks are not owned by this project.
